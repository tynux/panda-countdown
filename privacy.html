Privacy Policy — Panda Countdown

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “Panda Countdown”

English简体中文繁體中文

English

Your countdowns stay on your device. There is no account and no sign-in. The free version is supported by one small banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob, described in the Advertising section below.

What the app stores

Your countdowns, notes, intentions and preferences are stored only on your device:

  • inside the app’s own container, and
  • in a shared container (App Group) so the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets can display the same countdowns.

Both containers live on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to us unless you turn on cloud sync.

Cloud sync (optional, off until you turn it on)

If you sign in with Apple, your data is also stored on our backend so it stays in sync across your devices:

  • What: the countdowns and intentions you created, their notes, date and colour settings, any photo notes you attached, and an Apple user identifier.
  • Where: Cloudflare (Workers, a D1 database and R2 object storage).
  • Why: only to sync your own data between your own devices. It is never used for advertising, profiling or tracking, and it is never sold or shared.
  • Deleting it: Settings → Cloud sync → “Delete account and cloud data” erases everything on the server and signs you out. Signing out alone leaves the server copy in place.

If you never sign in, nothing is ever uploaded — the app stays entirely on your device.

Advertising

The app shows one banner ad at the bottom of the screen, supplied by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure it, Google may process:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking;
  • non-identifying device and connection details (device model, OS version, language, IP address, approximate region).

On first launch iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline — or if tracking is switched off system-wide in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking — the app requests non-personalised ads only and your advertising identifier is not used to personalise them. Every feature works exactly the same either way, and you can change the choice at any time in iOS Settings.

We never receive this data ourselves. Google handles it under its own policy: how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Advertising is the only third-party content in the app; there are no analytics SDKs, no trackers of our own and no data brokers.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications through iOS. They are created and delivered entirely on your device. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Panda Countdown.

Network

Everything except sync and advertising works offline. The app makes network requests only to load the banner ad and, if you enabled it, to sync your data.

Deleting your data

Settings → Data → “Erase everything” removes all countdowns, notes and intentions from the device. Deleting the app also removes all of its data. To reset the advertising identifier, use iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → “Allow Apps to Request to Track”.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested in non-personalised mode unless the user explicitly allows tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Email peill@me.com.

简体中文

你的倒计时只留在你的设备上。没有账号、不需要登录。免费版通过 Google AdMob 提供的一条小横幅广告支持运营，详见下面的「广告」一节。

App 保存了什么

你的倒计时、备忘录、心愿单和设置只保存在本机：

  • App 自己的沙盒目录；
  • 一个共享目录（App Group），用于让桌面小组件和锁屏小组件显示同样的倒计时。

这两个目录都在你的 iPhone 上。除非你主动开启云同步，否则不会上传给我们。

云同步（可选，默认关闭）

如果你用 Apple 登录，数据还会保存在我们的后端，以便在你的多台设备之间同步：

  • 存什么：你创建的倒计时与心愿、它们的备注、日期与颜色设置、你添加的图片备注，以及一个 Apple 用户标识符。
  • 存在哪：Cloudflare（Workers、D1 数据库与 R2 对象存储）。
  • 为什么：仅用于在你自己的设备之间同步你的数据。绝不用于广告、画像或追踪，也绝不出售或共享。
  • 怎么删：「设置 → 云同步 → 注销账号并删除云端数据」会清除服务器上的全部内容并退出登录。仅退出登录不会删除服务器副本。

如果你从不登录，就不会有任何上传，App 完全留在你的设备上。

广告

App 底部会显示一条横幅广告，由 Google AdMob 提供。为了投放和统计这条广告，Google 可能会处理：

  • 你设备的广告标识符（IDFA）——仅当你允许追踪时；
  • 不含身份信息的设备与网络信息（机型、系统版本、语言、IP 地址、大致地区）。

首次启动时 iOS 会询问是否允许 App 跨其他公司的 App 与网站追踪你。如果你选择「要求 App 不要追踪」，或在系统「设置 → 隐私与安全 → 追踪」中关闭了追踪，App 只会请求非个性化广告，你的广告标识符不会被用于个性化。无论选哪一个，App 的所有功能都完全一样，你也可以随时在系统设置里更改。

我们本人不会收到这些数据，它们由 Google 按其自身政策处理：Google 如何使用接入其服务的 App 中的信息。广告是 App 中唯一的第三方内容；我们没有接入任何统计 SDK，也没有自建的追踪或数据买卖。

通知

如果你开启了提醒，App 会通过 iOS 安排本地通知，完全在本机生成和送达。你可以随时在「设置 → 通知 → 熊猫倒数日」中关闭。

网络

除同步与广告外，所有功能都可离线使用。App 只在加载横幅广告时，以及在你开启了云同步时，才会发起网络请求。

删除数据

「设置 → 数据 → 清空全部数据」会删除本机所有倒计时、备忘录与心愿；删除 App 也会一并删除其数据。想重置广告标识符，可在系统「设置 → 隐私与安全 → 追踪 → 允许 App 请求追踪」中操作。

儿童

App 年龄分级为 4+，不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。除非用户明确允许追踪，广告始终以非个性化模式请求。

变更

如果本政策发生变化，更新后的版本会发布在本页并标注新的日期。

联系方式

有任何问题请邮件联系 peill@me.com。
