Privacy Policy — Panda Countdown

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “Panda Countdown”

English简体中文繁體中文

English

Panda Countdown does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

What the app stores

Your countdowns, notes, intentions and preferences are stored only on your device:

  • inside the app’s own container, and
  • in a shared container (App Group) so the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets can display the same countdowns.

Both containers live on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications through iOS. They are created and delivered entirely on your device. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Panda Countdown.

Network

The app does not make network requests. It works fully offline.

Deleting your data

Settings → Data → “Erase everything” removes all countdowns, notes and intentions from the device. Deleting the app also removes all of its data.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Email peill@me.com.

熊猫倒数日不收集你的任何数据。没有账号、没有登录、没有统计、没有广告，也没有接入任何第三方 SDK。

  • App 自己的沙盒目录；

删除数据

「设置 → 数据 → 清空全部数据」会删除本机所有倒计时、备忘录与心愿；删除 App 也会一并删除其数据。
